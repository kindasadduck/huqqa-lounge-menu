About Huqqa Lounge

A Turkish kitchen, hookah, tea and coffee in Falls Church, Virginia.

Start at the table.

Breakfast to share. Mezes to start with. Grilled kebabs, pide and something sweet to finish. Our menu brings these Turkish dishes together in one place.

Come for a meal, or take your time over tea and coffee. There’s room in the menu for either kind of visit.

Explore the menu

Stay for a while.

Food isn’t the whole visit. Choose a hookah from the menu, order a Turkish coffee or a glass of tea, and settle into the conversation.

For current hookah flavors, browse the menu or ask your server. Planning an evening with friends? Call us to reserve.
